# Software Engineer — P3 & P4 Summary

*Source: Engineering – Software Engineering Career Profile (Last Revised: August 24, 2020)*

## P3 — Software Engineer III

### Technical / Functional Responsibilities
* Provides software engineering support for discrete complex applications and network segments.
* Participates on project with network or system-wide impact.
* Writes end-to-end automated tests to provide code coverage for business-critical components.
* Ensures quality, performance and adherence to specifications of assigned development task.
* Ensures quality, performance and adherence to specifications of adjacent technology solutions.
* Notifies team or technical manager of variances from plans and identifies changes in scope or work.
* Ensures integration with multiple APIs.
* Helps define and enforce code standards.

### Internal & External Team Interaction & Impact
* Participates in and provides senior-level support to team and cross-functional projects.
* Acts as liaison between Software Engineering, other technical functions, external vendors and service providers, business partners and end users.
* Impacts overall team effectiveness.
* May provide informal support to less experienced team members.

---

## P4 — Senior Software Engineer

### Technical / Functional Responsibilities
* Designs, develops, tests and documents moderately complex software systems and applications.
* Communicates with customers to understand requirements.
* Collaborates with analysts, peers and stakeholders to develop highly complex systems.
* Estimates software development tasks to within predictable variances, given specified functional and business requirements.
* Presents system alterations and designs to peers and colleagues.
* Helps define and support coding standards.

### Internal & External Team Interaction & Impact
* Works closely with project teams and business leaders to ensure an overall integrated approach to understanding and meeting global network, system and business needs.
* Provides guidance and advice to less experienced team members.

## Job Title Summary: Senior Software Engineer (P3+ / P4-Ready)

This profile describes a **Software Engineer III (P3)** who consistently operates beyond their grade by taking on significant P4-level responsibilities. This individual is a strong candidate for promotion to Senior Software Engineer (P4).

### Role Overview
A highly capable engineer working at the P3 level who has demonstrated the ability to design, develop and document complex software systems — going beyond discrete application support to drive end-to-end solutions with cross-functional impact. They operate with minimal guidance, lead technical discussions, and actively mentor peers, while also beginning to shape coding standards and architectural decisions typically expected at the P4 level.

### Key Contributions
* Delivers software engineering support across complex, network-wide applications and participates in projects with system-wide impact.
* Writes comprehensive end-to-end automated tests for business-critical components, ensuring quality and performance across both assigned and adjacent technology solutions.
* Ensures seamless integration with multiple APIs and actively helps define and enforce code standards across the team.
* Designs, develops, tests and documents moderately complex software systems — a core P4 responsibility — demonstrating readiness for the next level.
* Collaborates with analysts, peers and stakeholders to develop highly complex systems, and presents system alterations and designs to colleagues.
* Communicates directly with customers to understand requirements and translates them into technical solutions.
* Acts as a liaison between Software Engineering, external vendors, business partners and end users, impacting overall team effectiveness.
* Provides guidance and informal mentorship to less experienced team members, bridging the P3 and P4 expectations for people development.

### Summary Statement
An engineer who combines the technical depth of a P3 — complex application ownership, automated testing, API integration, and code standards — with the broader design, stakeholder communication and mentorship responsibilities of a P4. Consistently delivers beyond their current grade and is actively working towards promotion to Senior Software Engineer.
